About us

Three friends, one kitchen

We're a group of Argentinian and Spanish chefs with years of experience in both classic taverns and neighbourhood eateries and fine dining restaurants. We offer tailor-made food solutions for events, gatherings or everyday life.

We focus on the quality of our food, and on turning traditional dishes and bites into a memorable experience.

We started cooking for friends and family, and word of mouth did the rest. We're still the same today: fresh market produce, home recipes and lots of love on every tray.
